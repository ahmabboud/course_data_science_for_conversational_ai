## 3. Knowledge errors and gaps

### Statements to correct

- **Module 5 "pass^k: succeeding once is not succeeding reliably."** The code comment says pass^1 means "succeeds at least once". That is wrong (that is pass@k). pass^1 is the chance one run succeeds, and pass^k is the chance all k runs succeed.
- **Module 5 "tau-bench, worked through."** It says "gpt-4o-class agents, retail domain, pass^1 below 50%". As I recall the paper's abstract, "below 50%" is across the tasks and "pass^8 below 25%" is for retail. The retail pass^1 for gpt-4o is higher. The tau-bench PDF is not in `research/`, so I could not check. Download it and confirm.
- **Module 5 "Deployment concerns."** It says "a p50 average". p50 is the median, not an average.
- **Module 5 "One number lies."** The 95% to 77% to 21% arithmetic is right (0.95^5 = 0.774, 0.95^30 = 0.215), but it assumes the stages fail independently. Say so in one line.
- **Module 5 "What regional regulation actually requires."** Says "Today Sept 2026". It is now October 2026. The Article 50 date and the status of the Digital Omnibus deferral are law that is still moving; confirm both against the official text before teaching.
- **Module 4 "Compaction" (SeCom numbers).** The numbers are correct (full history 54.15, SeCom 71.57, difference 17.4, and "up to 9.46" in the ablation). The framing misleads in two ways. First, the 17.4 gain is over reading the whole transcript; the paper's simplest retrieval baseline (turn-level BM25) already scores 65.58, so SeCom's own contribution over it is about 6 points. Second, "costs 9.46 points back" is the paper's maximum over settings ("up to"), not the setting shown. Say both.
- **Module 2 "The evaluation: digital twins plus expert review."** The slide says agreement is "79 to 92%". The paper says 79 to 92% in the results and 76 to 92% in its conclusion. Use "about 76 to 92%".
