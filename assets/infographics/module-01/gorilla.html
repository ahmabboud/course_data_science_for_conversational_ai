What this paper does not claim
  • Scoped to one API call per request, not the multi-step tool chains a real agent often needs.
  • APIBench is entirely ML-model APIs (HuggingFace, TorchHub, TensorHub), not general-purpose REST APIs.
  • An honest, unresolved finding stated in the paper itself: GPT-3.5 hallucinates less than GPT-4 in their own tests, and the authors say plainly they do not fully know why (they guess RLHF plays a role).
  • Gorilla is a fine-tuned model. This course uses prompted function calling instead; the transferable lesson is the retrieval-aware idea, not the fine-tuning recipe itself.
