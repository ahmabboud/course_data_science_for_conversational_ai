What this paper does not claim
  • Every reported gain is relative to one specific legacy baseline (this bank's own IVR and manual triage), not a zero-automation or universal baseline.
  • The large-scale evaluation uses synthetic "digital twin" customers, not live customer traffic. The more conservative, human-validated figure (subject-matter experts testing the live agent) is a +16.0% accuracy gain, meaningfully lower than the synthetic-testing numbers, and the paper reports that gap openly rather than smoothing over it.
  • Human/automated-judge agreement is strong on objective criteria (79 to 92%) but drops to around 60% on subjective ones like empathy and frustration, an explicitly named, unresolved weakness.
  • Deployed in one regulated domain (UK banking fraud, scam, and dispute triage). The paper makes no claim about transfer to a materially different domain.
